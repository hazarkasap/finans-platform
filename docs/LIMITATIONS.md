# Limitations

- Local educational showcase; not production ready and not financial advice.
- TRY is the only supported currency; no exchange rates or bank integrations.
- No email verification, password recovery, rate limiting, or multi-factor authentication.
- No automated recurring bills, notifications, import, export, or deletion workflow.
- The local API demo bypass must never be enabled in production.
- Deployment, backups, monitoring, incident response, and regulatory compliance are out of scope.
